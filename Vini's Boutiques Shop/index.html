Our Story

Stitched with Passion

Vini's Boutique was born out of a deep love for South Indian textile artistry. Founded in Hosur by master tailor Nithya Kumar, our boutique has spent over 4 years transforming fabric into wearable art — one stitch at a time.

From intricate Aari work passed down through generations to contemporary designer blouses, we blend tradition with modern elegance. Every customer who walks through our door leaves wearing confidence.

  • Hosur's Most Trusted — Serving 500+ happy clients across Hosur and beyond
  • Custom Fit Guaranteed — We take precise measurements for the perfect silhouette
  • Premium Materials — Only the finest threads, beads, and fabrics
  • Ready in Time — We honour every delivery commitment, especially for weddings
  • WhatsApp Orders — Easy booking, tracking updates and design consultation online
