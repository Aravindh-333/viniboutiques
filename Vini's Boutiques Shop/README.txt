=========================================================
  STEP-BY-STEP: HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE
=========================================================

STEP 1 — Change your personal info
   Open: pages/CHANGE_MY_INFO.txt
   Edit all your details there, then copy them into index.html

STEP 2 — Upload your photos
   Just copy your photos into the right folder:
   - Your work photos    → images/gallery/
   - Owner/shop photo    → images/about/
   - Banner/hero photo   → images/hero/
   (Name them exactly as shown above!)

STEP 3 — Open index.html in your browser to preview

STEP 4 — To go LIVE, upload the entire "priyasboutique" folder to:
   - Netlify.com (FREE, drag & drop)
   - Or any web hosting like Hostinger, GoDaddy
